## What Lys replaces, and what it does not (Tom, 12 Oct 22:2x)

Lys is not responsible for all of Argus. Every service is moving to run as a liminal service, so stream analytics (Argus's cells, dashboards, queue timelines, the MCP client registry, rules that count and warn on events) happen through liminal. Everything about context management, goals, budgets, words, schedules, variables, seats and their sessions, and the alarms that say a seat has stopped moving, goes through Lys. The Aion queue is Cambium's to call directly. Rewrite and continue rules are not carried: Lys's judge denies; goals and schedules nudge.
