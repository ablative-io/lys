# Decisions-Words — User Stories

## Ledger reader — Reading a decision

**S1.** As a ledger reader, I want each decision's quote to state the decision in the team's words so that the ledger holds no person's spoken words.

**S2.** As a ledger reader, I want a decision's context, decision and consequences to name roles instead of people, times of day and personal machines so that the ledger holds nothing personal.

## Brief author — Anchoring a decision

**S3.** As a brief author anchoring a decision, I want its decision, context and consequences to mean exactly what was ruled so that a review against the ledger checks the same obligations as before.

## Card lead — Landing a document change

**S4.** As a card lead landing the change, I want the design gate to pass with the schema untouched so that the ledger stays valid under the method it is measured by.
