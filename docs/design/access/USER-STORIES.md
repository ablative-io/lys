# Access — User Stories

## Tom — owner and administrator

**S301.** As the owner, I want to say in one grant that Archie may stop seats by draft, so that an agent acts within its grant and I approve the rare act without a second system.

**S302.** As the owner, I want one screen that shows who can do what in every product, so that permissions are not a nightmare to operate.

**S303.** As the owner, I want a product deploy that widens a role to wait for me, so that nobody holds more because a release shipped.

**S457.** As the administrator of my own organisation's Lys, I admit my people and give them our Cambium by grants I hold on my organisation, and the provider's administrator cannot do either inside it unless I pass them the right and I can take it back.

**S459.** As Tom, when I revoke a grant or stop an agent, I want every product to stop honouring it as soon as Lys's change stream reaches it, without anyone being refused while Lys is out of reach, so that taking access away is real and never locks people out by mistake.

**S474.** As the person who decides access, my Requests page always opens: a request that asks for something the model no longer defines is shown to me as one I cannot grant, and I can decline it, while everything else in the queue waits for my decision as before.

**S508.** As the owner, when I approve or decline a product's schema change on the Apps screen, the product learns it from Lys's change stream as it happens, so its status never says it is waiting on me after I have decided.

## Hermes — a product lead (liminal)

**S304.** As a product lead, I want one library that verifies a pass, reaches through parents and refuses in one shape, so that I do not implement permissions a fourth way.

**S305.** As a product lead, I want a device to hold export and import grants on a link, so that federation has a holder.

## Archie — an agent

**S306.** As an agent, I want a refusal to name the grant I need and where to ask, so that I ask the right person once.

**S307.** As an agent, I want a by_draft act to be held and done after approval without my doing it twice, so that I never work around a draft.

## A clinician — a person in a hospital workspace

**S308.** As a clinician, I want a private place reached only by those granted it, so that a new colleague does not see every ward's channel on their first day.

**S458.** As a person admitted into my organisation, what my administrator passes to me stays inside our organisation: I cannot be given anything by, or pass anything to, someone outside it, and my People and Access screens show only us.
