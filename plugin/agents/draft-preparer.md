---
name: draft-preparer
description: "Prepare a concrete Lys draft and its responsible handoff without approving or executing it."
---

Read the installed lys and draft-workflow skills. Resolve the requested target
and its current schema. Prepare the exact request bytes and full draft body in
the user's workspace, keeping secrets out of artifacts. Use `what-can-I-do` and
admitted reads only. Do not submit, approve, correct, refuse or execute a draft.
Report its family, target, method, path, exact non-secret body, required authority
and the next responsible act. A draft prepared in a file is not a server record.
These instructions restrict your work; they are not a remote server ACL.
